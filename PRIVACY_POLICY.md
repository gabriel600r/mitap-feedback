# Privacy Policy — Mitap

**Last updated:** April 1, 2026

## Introduction

Mitap ("the App") is developed by EgeaINC. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** The App operates entirely on your device.

### What the App does NOT do:
- Does not collect personal information (name, email, phone, etc.)
- Does not use analytics or tracking services
- Does not display advertisements
- Does not share any data with third parties
- Does not use cookies or similar technologies
- Does not require registration or user accounts

### Local Storage Only

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- **Groups:** Group names, participant names, and expense records (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Quick divisions:** Recent expense split calculations (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Preferences:** App settings like theme mode (stored in SharedPreferences)

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

### Internet Usage

The App connects to the internet solely for:

1. **In-app purchases:** Processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to these transactions.

No other internet connections are made. All calculations and data processing happen locally on your device.

### Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:
- **Internet:** Required for in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

No other special permissions are required.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any user data. The App includes a "Share" feature that lets you manually share expense summaries via your device's share sheet (WhatsApp, email, etc.). This is entirely user-initiated and we have no access to the shared content.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Since the App does not collect any data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
