# Privacy Policy — Mitap

**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

## Introduction

Mitap ("the App") is developed by EgeaINC. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

## Your Expenses Stay on Your Device

**EgeaINC does not collect, store, or receive your expense data.** Groups, names and amounts are stored only on your device.

The App stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- **Groups:** Group names, participant names, and expense records (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Quick divisions:** Recent expense split calculations (stored in local SQLite database)
- **Preferences:** App settings like theme mode (stored in the local database)

This data never leaves your device unless you share it yourself, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Users with Mitap Pro do not see ads, and the App does not start the ads service for them.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:
- The device's advertising ID
- Approximate location derived from the IP address
- Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
- Ad interactions (impressions and taps)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by EgeaINC. Your expense data (groups, names, amounts) is never shared with AdMob.

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Your choices:**
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Ajustes → Privacidad de anuncios.
- Buying Mitap Pro (one-time purchase) removes all ads.

## Internet Usage

The App connects to the internet for:

1. **In-app purchases:** Processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not have access to your payment information. Google's privacy policy applies to these transactions.
2. **Ads (free version only):** Loading ads from Google AdMob, as described above.

All calculations happen locally on your device, and the App works offline.

## Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:
- **Internet:** Required for in-app purchases through Google Play and for loading ads in the free version.
- **Advertising ID:** Used by Google AdMob to show ads in the free version.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your expense data. The App includes a "Share" feature that lets you manually share expense summaries and PDF exports via your device's share sheet (WhatsApp, email, etc.). This is entirely user-initiated and we have no access to the shared content.

### Sharing a group (link or QR)

You can share a group with other people as a link or a QR code. The group (its name, participants' names, expenses and payments) travels **inside the link itself**, after the "#" sign. Browsers never send that part to a server, so the group does not pass through EgeaINC's servers: the page at egeainc.com/mitap/g/ only hands the link to the App or points to Google Play. Anyone who has the link can see the group, so share it only with the people it belongs to.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.
